What this estimate includes — and what it doesn't

  • Included: 2026 federal income-tax brackets and standard deduction; Social Security (6.2% up to the 2026 wage base) and Medicare (1.45% + 0.9% above $200k/$250k); each state's 2026 income-tax brackets, standard deduction and personal exemption (Tax Foundation).
  • Not included: local income taxes (they matter in Maryland, New York City, and many Ohio, Pennsylvania, Indiana, Kentucky and Michigan localities), tax credits, itemized deductions, pre-tax benefits (401k, health premiums), capital gains, and self-employment tax.
  • States that allow deducting federal taxes or use unusual bases (e.g., Alabama, Louisiana, Missouri, Montana, Oregon) are approximated with their published brackets — actual liability there can differ.
  • The “adjusted for local prices” column divides take-home pay by the state's BEA cost-of-living index — a rough gauge of what the money buys locally, not a tax figure.
  • Estimates for comparison only — not tax advice.

Tax parameters: Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets (2026); IRS 2026 inflation adjustments. Cost-of-living index: BEA Regional Price Parities. Estimates, not advice.
