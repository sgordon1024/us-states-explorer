                                Vehicle & travel rules

                                Rest-area, inspection and public-land rules vary widely by state

                                29 states allow overnight parking at public highway rest areas; 7 prohibit it, and the rest post time limits. 38 jurisdictions require no periodic vehicle safety inspection. Federal public land — where dispersed camping is generally permitted — ranges from 0.3% of the state to 80.1%.

                                  Boating rules

                                  Five states require no boater education; ten require it for all operators

                                  Alaska, Arizona, Idaho, South Dakota and Wyoming have no mandatory boater education. Most states use age-based cutoffs that exempt many adults. Ten jurisdictions require certification for every operator. Non-motorized canoes and kayaks must be registered in Ohio and DC, and need permits or stickers in seven other states.
